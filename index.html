About & Experience

I am a final-year mechanical engineering student at the University of Southampton graduating in July 2027. I gained hands-on experience in vacuum-system design, vibration analysis, and CAD during an industrial placement at the STFC between July 2025 and July 2026. As part of the vibration analysis project, I had to develop procedural documentation for a vibration analyser as well as a Python script with an in-built GUI to analyse and graphically present large data sets for future users. During my time at university, I have held two leadership roles in Formula Student which have demonstrated an ability to guide small teams, deliver lightweight composite structures informed through simulations, and produce clear technical documentation. I am proficient in Ansys Mechanical, Star CCM+, MATLAB, Python, and Excel and have used these extensively throughout my time at university. Outside of university, I am a keen triathlete. I have completed two Ironman 70.3 events in the past year with a PB of 5 hours 20 minutes. This test of endurance has built upon several soft skills that I look to carry over to my professional life.
